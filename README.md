## Taiwo Peter Akinremi

I research critical infrastructure cyber resilience, focusing on how water and wastewater systems maintain essential service during disruptions and recover afterward. At the University of Cincinnati, I use system dynamics, network science, control theory, and cybersecurity testbeds to study how infrastructure design and operating decisions affect resilience.

My work asks three questions that matter to utility leaders. Which dependencies put essential service at risk? Which interventions improve continuity and recovery? How can operators and executives tell whether those investments worked?

My research is complemented by Internet governance leadership as a Vice Chair of the ICANN GNSO Council, where I help guide policy work across stakeholder groups.
